# AI Marketing Playbook
### Practical AI Workflows for Digital Marketing Agencies & Small Businesses

**By Chad @ [Cube Creative Design](https://cubecreative.design)** — Partner & Chief "Smarketing" Officer

---

## What This Is

This is a collection of real-world AI workflows, Claude Project configurations, prompt templates, and automation setups that I use every day to run a digital marketing agency. No theory. No fluff. Just the stuff that actually works.

I built these over 2+ years of integrating AI into agency operations — from blog content pipelines to client onboarding to competitive analysis. If you're a marketer, agency owner, or small business operator trying to figure out how AI fits into your workflow, you're in the right place.

## Who I Am

I run marketing operations at Cube Creative Design, a digital agency specializing in pest control companies and K-12 private schools. I've been in digital marketing for 25+ years and started using AI tools the day ChatGPT launched. I'm also the author of *Digital Marketing for Rural Small Businesses* and a regular speaker on practical AI applications at industry conferences.
