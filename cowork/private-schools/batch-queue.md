## SECTION 4: Processing Notes

### How to Use This Example
This batch queue demonstrates the format for planning a full month of content around a seasonal theme (retention and re-enrollment). When building your own queue:

1. **Choose a seasonal theme** using `reference/seasonal-calendar.md`
2. **Identify 10-15 topics** that cluster around that theme
3. **Assign writer personas** based on topic complexity (data-heavy vs. approachable how-to)
4. **Assign reader personas** from your own persona set in `reference/k-12-private-school-personas.md`
5. **Mix content goals** — not every post should be lead generation
6. **Vary word counts** — include pillar content (5,000 words) alongside shorter supporting posts (2,000 words)
7. **Add research links** in the Research/Notes column if you have Google Deep Research docs or other source material

### Gaps & Flags to Watch For
1. **Pillar content:** Mark your hub posts. All other entries in the cluster should link back during Stage 6.
2. **Keyword cannibalization risk:** Watch for overlapping primary keywords. Differentiate during Stage 1.
3. **Cross-month linking:** Connect new posts to relevant content from previous months during Stage 6.
