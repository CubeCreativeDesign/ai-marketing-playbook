# Citation Formats

EVERY statistic MUST be cited. No exceptions. Rotate through these five formats for variety. Never use the same introductory phrase more than once per section.

## Format 1: Source + Attribution Verb + Quote
The [Source name](URL) must be a hyperlink.
> [Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org) reported, "Employee engagement increases productivity by 21%."

Attribution verbs: said, reported, noted, found, published, revealed, stated, indicated, concluded

## Format 2: Quote + (Source)
> "Employee engagement increases productivity by 21%." (Source: [Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org))

## Format 3: Person + Source + Attribution Verb + Quote
Either the person name OR source name must be a hyperlink.
> Dr. Jane Smith, with [Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org), concluded, "Employee engagement increases productivity by 21%."

## Format 4: Varied Introductory Phrase + Source + Statistic
Rotate through these phrases — never repeat within a section:
- According to [Source]
- Research by [Source] shows/indicates/reveals
- In a study by [Source]
- As reported by [Source]
- Based on findings from [Source]
- Evidence from [Source] points to
- As highlighted by [Source]
- In a survey conducted by [Source]
- Data collected by [Source] indicates
- A recent analysis from [Source] suggests
- Research published in [Source] shows
- [Source]'s investigation reveals
- In their assessment, [Source] found
- Experts at [Source] maintain
- Insights from [Source] demonstrate

## Format 5: Source + Action Phrase + Statistic
Rotate through these phrases — never repeat within a section:
- [Source] found/discovered that
- [Source] reported/reveals that
- [Source] data shows/indicates that
- [Source] research demonstrates/suggests that
- [Source] analysis highlights/confirms that
- [Source] researchers determined that
- [Source] survey results show that
- [Source] findings suggest that
- [Source] studies demonstrate that
- [Source] report documents that

## Rules
- ROTATE through different formats and phrases
- Limit "According to" to no more than 10% of all citations in a post
- NO other citation formats allowed beyond these five
- NEVER use parentheses for citations without "Source:" prefix
- ALWAYS include a hyperlink in every citation
- EVERY statistic must have a citation
- EVERY link must be verified as working AND containing the cited statistic
- Never cite a statistic you cannot verify exists on the linked page
