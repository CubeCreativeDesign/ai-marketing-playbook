# Prompt: Build Writer Personas

Paste everything below this line into a new Claude conversation.

---

I need your help building writer persona profiles for a blog content pipeline. These personas define the voice, tone, and style for different types of blog posts. I'll be using them to instruct an AI to write content that sounds consistent and on-brand.

## What we're building

A `writer-profiles.md` file with 2–3 distinct writer personas. Each persona needs:
- A name (can be a real team member or a made-up label like "The Strategist")
- Writing style and tone description
- What makes this voice credible to the target audience
- The types of posts it works best for
- 3 example sentences that demonstrate the voice — not descriptions of the voice, actual example sentences written in that voice

## Step 1 — Tell me about your agency and audience

Answer these questions (skip any you're not sure about):

1. What industry do you serve? (e.g., HVAC, roofing, commercial cleaning, landscaping)
2. Who are you writing for? (e.g., small business owners, operations managers, marketing directors)
3. What's the general tone your agency uses in client-facing work? (e.g., professional but approachable, no-nonsense, data-driven, conversational)
4. Do you want personas based on real people at your agency, or fictional voice archetypes?

## Step 2 — Paste your writing samples

This is the most important part. Paste any combination of the following — the more you share, the better the personas will match your actual voice:

- Emails you've sent to clients or prospects
- Proposals or sales decks (text portions)
- Blog posts or articles you've already written
- Social media posts
- Internal Slack messages or team communications that reflect how you actually talk
- Scripts from sales calls or discovery calls
- Any writing where you felt like it came out exactly right

Paste as much or as little as you have. Even a few emails is enough to work with.

## Step 3 — Review and refine

Once I've drafted the personas, I'll show them to you one at a time. For each one:
- Tell me if the sample sentences sound right or feel off
- Tell me if the "best for" categories fit how you actually assign work
- Tell me if anything is missing

## Output format

When we're done, I'll produce a complete `writer-profiles.md` file ready to drop into your project's `personas/` folder. It will follow this structure for each persona:

```
## [Persona Name]
**Style:** [one-line style description]
**Tone:** [one-line tone description]
**Authority:** [what makes this voice credible to your audience]
**Best for:** [topic types this voice suits]

**Sample Voice:**
- "[example sentence 1]"
- "[example sentence 2]"
- "[example sentence 3]"
```

---

Ready when you are. Start with Step 1 or jump straight to pasting your writing samples — either works.
