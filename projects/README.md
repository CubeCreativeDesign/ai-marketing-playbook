# Claude Project Templates

Paste-in Claude Project setups. Each one gives you the Custom Instructions, how to use it, and tips for making it yours.

## How to Use These

1. **Create a new Project** in Claude (click "Projects" in the sidebar).
2. **Copy the Custom Instructions** from the template's code block into your Project's instructions.
3. **Upload any knowledge files** the template suggests, or your own versions.
4. **Start chatting.** Claude follows the workflow on its own.

## Available Templates

| Template | What It Does | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| [Titles, Meta & Keywords](titles-meta-keywords.md) | SEO title packages, meta descriptions, and keywords from any blog post. The SEO title sells the click; the H1 confirms the reader is in the right place. | Content marketers, SEO specialists, blog managers |
| [Check Links](check-links.md) | Verifies every citation and link before you publish. A stat only counts as verified when the number is on the page. | Content editors, blog managers, anyone publishing sourced content |
| [Create or Optimize Prompts](create-optimize-prompts.md) | Builds better prompts, structured with proven frameworks. Interviews you one question at a time, then writes the prompt. | Anyone building Claude Projects or writing system prompts |
| [Prompt Check](prompt-check.md) | Grades any prompt against the Trust Insights frameworks and hands back a fixed version. Also builds Deep Research prompts. | Anyone with prompts they reuse every week |

**Create or Prompt Check?** Create builds a new prompt from scratch. Prompt Check grades one you already have.

## A Note on Customization

These are starting points. The best Claude Projects are the ones you've tweaked to match your voice, your clients, and your process. Don't just copy and paste. Read through each one and ask, "What would make this work better for my business?" Then swap in one real example from your own work. That single change usually does more than anything else.
